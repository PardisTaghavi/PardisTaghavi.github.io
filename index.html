Selected Publications

Video Generation & World Models

Partition the Support, Reconstruct the Residual: Training-Free Sparse Attention for Video Generation and World Models

Pardis Taghavi, Reza Langari, Gaurav Pandey

arXiv, 2026 · Under review

Approximates dense attention with sparse computation and residual correction, accelerating video generators and world models without retraining. Achieves 1.48–2.61× end-to-end speedups while preserving generation quality.

Paper·Webpage

ActionSplice: In-Flight Action Editing for Interactive World Models

Pardis Taghavi, Tingyu Guo, Jonas Lossner, Gaurav Pandey, Reza Langari

arXiv, 2026 · Under review

Enables users to revise actions during world-model generation without restarting sampling. A lightweight corrector updates intermediate representations to reflect the new actions, supporting both whole-chunk changes and edits that preserve an existing prefix.

Paper·Webpage

The Pulse of Motion: Measuring Physical Frame Rate from Visual Dynamics

Xiangbo Gao, Mingyang Wu, Siyuan Yang, Jiongze Yu, Pardis Taghavi, Fangzhou Lin, Zhengzhong Tu

arXiv, 2026 · Under review

Introduces physical frame rate, a measure of whether video motion unfolds at a physically plausible speed. A learned visual chronometer estimates this rate, enabling playback corrections that improve the perceived naturalness of generated motion.

Paper·Webpage

Visual Perception & Autonomous Driving

SwinMTL: A Shared Architecture for Simultaneous Depth Estimation and Semantic Segmentation from Monocular Camera Images

Pardis Taghavi, Reza Langari, Gaurav Pandey

IROS, 2024

Jointly estimates depth and semantic segmentation from a single image using a shared transformer architecture. Adversarial training refines both predictions and improves performance on Cityscapes and NYU Depth V2.

Paper·Code

NaviDriveVLM: Decoupling High-Level Reasoning and Motion Planning for Autonomous Driving

Ximeng Tao, Pardis Taghavi, Dimitar Filev, Reza Langari, Gaurav Pandey

arXiv, 2026 · Under review

Separates high level driving reasoning from motion planning. A large vision language model guides a lightweight driving model through an interpretable intermediate representation. This design reduces adaptation cost and improves planning performance on nuScenes.

Paper·Code

Training a Student Expert via Semi-Supervised Foundation Model Distillation

Pardis Taghavi, Tian Liu, Renjie Li, Reza Langari, Zhengzhong Tu

CVPR Workshops, 2026

Distills foundation models into a compact instance segmentation model using limited labeled data. Teacher adaptation and contrastive learning reduce pseudo label bias. The student is approximately 11× smaller and outperforms its adapted teachers.
